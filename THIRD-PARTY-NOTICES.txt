Third-party components included with the Windows installer

FFmpeg and ffprobe
------------------
The installer includes FFmpeg 7.1.1 essentials for Windows from gyan.dev.
That build is licensed under GNU GPL version 3. Its license and build
configuration are included with the installer.

FFmpeg source:
https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/db69d06eee
Build provider:
https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
